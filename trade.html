Trade Rules

  • When to trade: 1-2 years left on contract, in prime of career, can help struggling team rebuild for future
  • When NOT to trade: When team is trying to make the playoffs, or if deal price is too high
  • Good trade: 1 superstar player goes to a good team in return for 3-4 young players or prospects
  • Bad trade: 1 young superstar player goes to a good team in return for a 40 year old pitcher
  • Trade deadline: During the season is usually around July 31st or occasionally on August 1st
